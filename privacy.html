Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · AI Listing Assistant / Inventory Manager AI

This policy describes how the Chrome extension AI Listing Assistant (including Inventory Status) handles information.

1. Data we access

  • Google account email — when you sign in with Google (Inventory / Sheets).
  • Google Sheets — only spreadsheets you connect; we read/write rows you request via the extension.
  • Product images & listing fields — processed on marketplace pages to generate listing content.
  • API keys you enter — stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage), not on our servers.
  • Account / subscription data — if you use our optional login (e.g. Supabase), email and plan status as needed for access control.

2. How we use data

  • To fill listing forms under your review.
  • To manage inventory, sales, and bills in your Google Sheet.
  • To call AI providers with your own API keys when you generate content.

3. Storage & sharing

  • Listing and settings data primarily stay on your device.
  • Sheet data stays in your Google account.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Payment providers (e.g. Razorpay) process payments under their own policies when you buy a plan.

4. Third parties

Depending on features you use: Google (OAuth, Sheets), AI providers you configure (Gemini, OpenAI, etc.), Supabase (auth/subscription), Razorpay (payments).

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google in Inventory settings.
  • Revoke access: Google Account → Third-party access.
  • Remove the extension to delete local extension storage.
